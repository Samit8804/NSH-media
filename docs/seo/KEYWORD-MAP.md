# Keyword and Query Map — NSH Media Services

- **Canonical host:** https://nsh-media.vercel.app
- **Prepared:** 12 November 2026
- **Scope:** 22 indexable URLs in the sitemap
- **Data limitation:** No paid keyword tool, Search Console export, or search-volume data was available. Priorities below are based on commercial intent, existing page structure, and the three verified services. They are not volume forecasts.

---

## 1. Strategy

The site should compete for **commercial website-design and development queries** first, then support them with **industry-specific long-tail pages** and **answer-first informational content**.

Three rules govern the map:

1. **One primary theme per URL.** Service pages own commercial service terms. Industry pages own sector-qualified terms. Blog posts own informational questions. Pricing owns cost queries.
2. **Only verified services are targeted.** No pages or keyword targeting for SEO services, branding, AI automation, or standalone maintenance.
3. **No location targeting yet.** There is no verified address or service area, so the site does not target “near me,” city, or country modifiers. Add location pages only after the business confirms a service area.

## 3. Keyword clusters

### 3.1 Commercial service cluster

| Cluster | Owning URL | Supporting pages |
|---|---|---|
| Web design services | `/services/web-design` | `/services`, `/portfolio`, `/pricing`, industry pages |
| Web development services | `/services/web-development` | `/services`, `/portfolio`, `/pricing`, industry pages |
| E-commerce development | `/services/ecommerce` | `/services`, `/portfolio`, `/pricing`, `/industries/e-commerce` |
| Website pricing and packages | `/pricing` | `/faq`, all three service pages |
| Portfolio and proof | `/portfolio` | `/about`, service pages, home |

### 3.2 Industry long-tail cluster

Each industry page targets one sector-qualified phrase and links back to the three service pages. Industry pages must not compete for the unqualified phrase “web design services.”

### 3.3 Informational and AEO cluster

The FAQ page currently owns the strongest answer queries. Planned blog content (see `CONTENT-PLAN.md`) should own question-style searches and link to exactly one commercial page per article.

Example question themes:

- How much does a website cost?
- How long does a website take to build?
- What is included in a website design project?
- Web design vs. web development: what is the difference?
- How do I choose an e-commerce platform?
- What should a business website include?
- How do I prepare content before a website project?
- What happens after a website launches?

### 3.4 Brand cluster

`NSH Media Services`, `NSH media`, and `Visual Solutions` are brand/entity terms owned by the home, about, and contact pages. The brand name is consistently `NSH Media Services` with `NSH media` as an alternate name.

### Service pages

| Question | Page | Schema |
|---|---|---|
| How long does a web design project take? | `/services/web-design` | FAQPage |
| Do you include responsive design? | `/services/web-design` | FAQPage |
| What technologies do you use? | `/services/web-development` | FAQPage |

| Which e-commerce platform do you use? | `/services/ecommerce` | FAQPage |
| Do you handle payment integration? | `/services/ecommerce` | FAQPage |

### Answer-quality rules

1. Put a direct answer in the first 40–60 words under the question heading.
2. Follow with scope limits, exceptions, or next steps.
3. Keep visible text and schema answers identical.
4. Do not add FAQ schema to pages where the question is not visible.
5. Do not answer with invented prices, timelines, or guarantees. Link to `/pricing` or `/contact` instead.

## 6. Cannibalization controls

1. **Pricing vs. FAQ:** `/pricing` owns commercial cost queries; `/faq` gives a short answer and links to pricing. Blog posts about cost must link to `/pricing` and avoid duplicating the package table.
2. **Service pages vs. industry pages:** Industry pages always qualify the keyword with the sector and link to the service page for the unqualified term.
3. **Home vs. services hub:** Home targets the brand plus the broad studio proposition; `/services` targets the service catalogue.
4. **Blog vs. service pages:** Blog posts answer questions and demonstrate expertise; service pages carry the commercial offer and calls to action.
5. **No duplicate titles or H1s.** The current build has unique titles and descriptions across all 22 sitemap URLs.

## 7. Terms intentionally excluded

| Excluded term or theme | Reason |
|---|---|
| SEO services, branding, AI automation, website maintenance | Not verified as standalone services |
| Location keywords (“near me,” city names, countries) | No verified address or service area |
| “Best agency,” “top-rated,” “award-winning” | Unsupported claims |
| Guaranteed rankings, traffic, or revenue | Cannot be substantiated |
| Specific review ratings or client counts | No verified reviews or counts |
| Competitor names | No verified comparison data |
| “Cheap” or discount-led terms | Inconsistent with published positioning and unverified discounting |

## 8. Measurement plan

Because no keyword data or Search Console access was available, baseline and progress must be measured after deployment:

- **Search Console:** impressions, clicks, CTR, average position by page and query; index coverage; Core Web Vitals.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools:** index coverage and query data.
- **Analytics:** organic sessions, service-page engagement, pricing views, contact-form submissions, email clicks.
- **AI referral tracking:** sessions from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, and Gemini referrers where available; treat as directional, not complete.
- **Rank tracking:** optional; use a small set of commercial and brand terms rather than a large vanity list.

Do not report ranking or citation outcomes until these systems have collected data.
